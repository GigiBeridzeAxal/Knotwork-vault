# Motion graphics films skill

A Claude skill for making studio-quality motion graphics films in code: a launch, pitch, product or explainer film drawn as a web page that is a pure function of time, rendered frame by frame, then given a voice, music and code-made sound effects. Gigi asked for it on 2026-11-27, built from what was learned on the lead 2 pitch films (a static version rejected, a dynamic rebuild called "perfect", a 92-second version with its own motion system accepted).

- Source: `60 Resources/Skills/motion-graphics-films/SKILL.md` (master copy; edit here, then re-save the skill).
- Kit: `60 Resources/Skills/motion-graphics-films/kit/` holds the reference engine, the kit page, the render, review and check tools, and the sound tools (voice tightening, effects, music fitting, mix).
- How the last film was made, step by step: [[Make a coded pitch film with voice and music]].

## What we learned
- The person's bar: never still, a new picture every 1 to 2 seconds, the camera going to the detail, big type, a brisk voice, music balanced under it.
- "Creative" came from a motion system built from the client's own design language, not from effects.
- Instagram DMs refuse videos over 25 MB and show the first frame as the preview: send a 720p encode with a cover frame first.
