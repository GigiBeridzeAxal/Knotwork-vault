# Client acquisition

The plan for turning the portfolio into paying clients. Proposed on 2026-11-15, waiting for Gigi's OK on the niche, the Upwork budget and the site changes. The visual plan (private artifact) is at the `url` above.

## Positioning
One thing to be known for: **MCP servers and AI agent integrations for software companies**. Line to use on every profile: "I build MCP servers and AI agents that are reliable enough to run without you watching. Live in one to two weeks, and close to $0 a month to run."

- Buyers: SaaS founders and small product teams with an API and no MCP server; AI and automation agencies needing extra hands; small businesses wanting one task automated.
- Proof: [[Rouse]] (live remote MCP server with OAuth), [[Knotwork]] (workflow engine on a free plan), production work on Gigr.
- Second line: automation for small businesses. Easier to sell on Upwork, lower prices, so it supports the niche and does not replace it.

## Offers and starting prices
Starting points from typical market ranges, not measured. First two or three jobs at the low end for reviews, then raise.

| Offer | Price | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Agent and automation audit (written plan and a short video; price comes off the build) | $150 to $300 | 2 to 3 days |
| **MCP server sprint** (main offer: 5 to 8 tools, OAuth, tests, docs, deployed, listed in directories) | $1,500 to $3,000 (first clients $800 to $1,200) | 1 to 2 weeks |
| One automation, end to end (n8n, Make or custom code) | $400 to $1,500 | 3 to 7 days |
| Agent MVP (sell after an audit or sprint; 50% up front) | $3,000 to $6,000 | 3 to 4 weeks |
| Care plan, offered at every handover | $300 to $800 a month | ongoing |
| Upwork hourly | $30 to $35, then $45 to $60 after 3 to 5 reviews | |

## Channels, in order
1. **Direct outreach** (LinkedIn and X messages): 10 a day to SaaS products with a public API and no MCP server; a tiny working demo for the best 2 a week. Free.
2. **Upwork**: profile rewritten around the niche; 2 to 3 proposals a day to fresh jobs with verified payment, opened with a short video. The only paid channel: Connects cost roughly $2 a proposal, about $60 to $100 a month. Needs Gigi's OK ([[Working agreements]]).
3. **Building in public**: three posts a week, Rouse in the MCP directories, Knotwork on Show HN and r/selfhosted.
4. **Agencies**: offer white-label overflow work to 20 small AI and automation agencies.
5. **Communities**: two helpful answers a day (MCP, Cloudflare, n8n), no pitching.
6. **Fiverr and Contra**: two fixed-price gigs, set once.

Getting paid does not wait for [[US LLC for Stripe]]: platforms pay out directly, direct clients can pay by invoice.

## Daily routine (about 90 minutes)
10 direct messages, 2 to 3 proposals, same-day replies, one post or two helpful answers.

Planning guesses for 30 days: 200 messages give about 16 replies, 6 calls, 1 to 2 clients; 50 proposals give about 8 replies, 4 interviews, 1 to 2 clients. Replace with real rates after week two.

## 30-day plan
- **Week 1 (16 to 22 Nov)**: rewrite Upwork, make LinkedIn, record two demo videos, list 100 prospects, start the routine.
- **Week 2 (23 to 29 Nov)**: routine daily, post the Rouse film, list Rouse in MCP directories, two prospect demos, 10 agencies.
- **Week 3 (30 Nov to 6 Dec)**: follow-ups, Knotwork on Show HN, one low-priced job for the first niche review, Fiverr and Contra gigs, 10 more agencies.
- **Week 4 (7 to 15 Dec)**: deliver early, ask for reviews, offer the care plan, drop the weakest channel, raise prices one step, write the first client case study.
