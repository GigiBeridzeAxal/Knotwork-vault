# ADR-0029 Lead with AI automation on Upwork, Fiverr and direct messages

## Context
The portfolio is in good shape and Gigi wants paying clients. The first plan (2026-11-15) needed one thing to lead with and a short list of channels.

## Options considered
1. Lead with MCP servers and AI agent integrations for software companies; direct outreach first, Upwork second. Fewer competitors and higher prices, but a smaller market and slower first money.
2. Lead with AI automation for businesses on Upwork and Fiverr, plus direct messages to US and local businesses. Bigger market and faster first jobs, but crowded and lower starting prices.

## Decision
Option 2, chosen by Gigi on 2026-11-16. MCP servers and custom agents stay as the specialist offer.

## Consequences
- Upwork Connects are a real cost (roughly $60 to $100 a month); platforms keep 10 to 20% of each job.
- Standing out depends on the reliability message and on demo videos, not on the category name.
- AI may draft and prepare, but proposals and messages are sent by Gigi. No bidding bots.
- Local clients pay less but give fast first case studies.
